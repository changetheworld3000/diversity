How to get Acess to UWM Data Databases

  1. Go to uwm.edu/data
  2. Under find campus data go to Published Data and Dashboards
  3. Under Office of Assessment and Institutional Research (OAIR) Reports click Need Access?
  4. Go to the Data Authorization Request Form.
    • System: Microsoft Power BI
    • Group: Free for Students License
    • Supervisor: Center Director, or Trustworthy Professor
  5. Your request must be "approved by your supervisor, the campus data custodians in the Registrar’s Office, and the administrators."
    Email air-web@uwm.edu, edm-team@uwm.edu. and your supervisor to expidite acess.
  6. After approved, you will have acess to the UWM Data Hub
